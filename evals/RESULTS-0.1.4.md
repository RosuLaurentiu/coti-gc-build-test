# v0.1.4 diagnostic replay results

Date: 2026-10-07. Scope: one independent offline forward check. No native action.

## Why the guidance changed

Recent saved build evidence exposed three reusable decisions:

- A separately authorized native phase succeeded above an observed endpoint
  estimation allowance. The earlier blanket stop wording needed an explicit
  distinction between a failed strict run and a new, bounded model-admission
  policy. Automatic fallback and unexplained reverts remain forbidden.
- A local expected model and observation helper omitted the same changing
  identity. A faithful compiled getter exposed the failure. The local repair
  retained strict accounting checks; native reconciliation was still open in
  the reviewed checkpoint.
- Measured recursive history validation dominated runner time. A content-bound
  reuse prototype passed focused local checks, but was not integrated into the
  active runner. Its component timing was not complete-run performance evidence.

The lead inspected exact source reports, terminal records and the relevant
model, reader and guard source. The source-project graph generation was stale
and candidate paths were untracked; direct source was the fallback. This is
bounded evidence, not an exhaustive codebase or runtime audit. Original reports,
private artifacts, addresses, journals and permissions remain in their project.

## Inputs and method

The [three raw cases](CASES-0.1.4.md) use synthetic identifiers, snippets and
numbers. They reduce the observed decision boundaries; they do not replay the
native transactions or copy the project's private source.

One independent agent used GPT-6 Astra, High, with no inherited conversation.
Allowed reads were the case file and a v0.1.4 guidance snapshot. It did not
receive the [predefined rubric](RUBRIC-0.1.4.md), expected answers, source-project
history or other evaluator outputs. The snapshot included SKILL.md, five
references, the unchanged example, and the prior v0.1.3 validation history.

The lead scored the [actual answer](results/v0.1.4.md); grading was not blind.
The answer is retained verbatim apart from its heading and trailing newline.
The evaluator did not execute its proposed corrections or tests.

## Limits

One forward run on information-rich cases does not establish a measured gain
over v0.1.3, general accuracy, saved tokens, lower build time or native safety.
The earlier replay cases were preserved but were not rerun with v0.1.4.
This update introduces no new native result for the teaching counter.

The build evidence supports the stated lessons within its recorded scope.
The local model repair did not itself reconcile a native deposit, and the local
history guard did not establish full validator integration or native latency.
Those remain work for their original project owners.
